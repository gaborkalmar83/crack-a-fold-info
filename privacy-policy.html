Crack A Fold

Privacy policy. Last updated: 20 November 2026.

Crack A Fold is a prank app for foldable Android phones: it shows a fake cracked screen when the phone is folded. It is made by Kalmarium Services BV, the Netherlands ("we").

In short: the app collects no personal data, has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and does not connect to the internet. Everything happens on your phone.

What the app uses on your phone

Hinge angle and motion sensors tell the app when the phone is being folded, so the prank can start at the right moment. The readings are used live and are not stored or sent anywhere.

Display over other apps lets the app draw the fake crack on top of whatever is on screen. It only draws; it cannot read what other apps show.

Screenshot mode (optional). If you choose "Capture home screen", Android asks you to allow screen capture. The app then takes one picture of your screen, stores it privately inside the app, and stops capturing immediately. If you pick an image instead, Android's photo picker gives the app only the one image you chose. The picture never leaves your phone and is deleted when you tap Remove or uninstall the app.

Sound, vibration and media volume. The crack sound is generated on your phone. If you allow it, the app raises the media volume for a moment and then puts it back.

Notifications show that the prank is armed and let you repair the screen.

Your settings are stored on your phone only.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your phone.
