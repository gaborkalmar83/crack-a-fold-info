Crack A Fold

Hand it over. Let them fold it. Watch their face.

A prank for foldable Android phones. Arm it, hand your phone to a friend, and the moment they fold it they hear a sharp crack, feel a jolt, and the big screen shows a broken crease. Nothing is actually damaged.

On your crease
The crack lands exactly on your phone's own hinge line.
Real failure looks
Hairline crack, black crease line, spreading ink, a dead half, stuck pixel lines.
Never the same sound
Every crack sound is generated on the spot, with a short haptic jolt.
You set the trap
Breaks when they close it or open it flat, on the 1st to 5th fold.
